# Projects Upload Directory

Drop your media files or project folders here to feature them on your portfolio website.
Whenever you add or change files, simply say **"updated"**, and your portfolio will automatically refresh with your new work!

## Folder Structure:
- `projects/advertisement/`
- `projects/generative-ai/`
- `projects/graphic-designs/`
- `projects/photography/`
- `projects/videography/`

## How to organize a project:
### Option 1: Multi-file project (Folder with images/videos):
Create a folder inside the category, e.g.:
`projects/photography/monochrome-series/`
Put your images (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`) or video (`.mp4`) inside it.
The portfolio will display them as physical polaroid prints on the flipped postcard!
Every photo will automatically preserve its exact original aspect ratio.

### Option 2: Single-file project (Direct video or image):
Drop a file directly, e.g.:
`projects/videography/brand-commercial.mp4`
The portfolio will play the video full-screen inside the flipped postcard.
